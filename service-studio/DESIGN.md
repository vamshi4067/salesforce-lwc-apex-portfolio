# Service Studio visual system

A practical field notebook for two Salesforce workflows. Slate ink (#263d4a), cool paper (#edf1f0), white working surfaces and a restrained terracotta accent (#a44d35). DM Sans is self-hosted. The quote resembles a working document, while the appointment flow emphasizes the available day and technician. Navigation uses a clear underline, with generous room around the actual work instead of ornamental cards. Preserve readable labels, visible focus, native form controls, inline errors, honest sample-data disclosures and responsive layouts.
